Cook Costello Match Play

It'll never topple the Race to Hanmer in our affections, but the Match Play is the proud second jewel in the society's crown, and the only one where you get to look your opponent in the eye!

Qualifying happens over the first three events of the year. Your best two Stableford scores are combined, and the top 16 head into the knockouts.
