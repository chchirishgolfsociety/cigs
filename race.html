Cook Costello Race to Hanmer

Think Race to Dubai, or the FedEx Cup - same idea, considerably fewer private jets and significantly more craic. The Race to Hanmer is our flagship competition: eleven events across courses in and around the city, with each player's best six stableford scores carried through to a running total. It all comes down to a final weekend away, usually somewhere far enough outside Christchurch so nobody can go home early.

We're grateful to Cook Costello for their continued support of the Race to Hanmer. Please consider them for any engineering, surveying or geophysics help.
