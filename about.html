About the society

The Christchurch Irish Golf Society brings together the Irish community around Canterbury — and plenty of honorary members besides — for a season of monthly social golf. It's less about the handicap and more about the craic: a good walk, a few laughs, and a pint afterwards.

Each season runs from summer through to a finale at Hanmer Springs in November, with scores from every round feeding the Race to Hanmer leaderboard. Courses have included Amberley, Harewood, Avondale, McLeans Island, Shirley, Kaiapoi, Bottle Lake, Templeton, and Terrace Downs.
