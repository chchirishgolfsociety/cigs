We play one round a month, on courses all over Christchurch and beyond. It's a good excuse to get off your home track (if you have one) and see what the rest of the city has to offer. Every event feeds into the Race leaderboard.

We negotiate group rates on green fees wherever we can, and with the support of Cook Costello we're usually able to bring them down further again. Our goal is to keep the entry fee as low as possible to encourage people to come out for a hit.
