Who we are

A round a month, and everyone's welcome

The Christchurch Irish Golf Society plays courses right across Canterbury each season — from Amberley to Hanmer Springs — with the season's scores feeding into the Race to Hanmer leaderboard. New members and visitors are always welcome on the tee sheet.

The Race

Race to Hanmer

Best six scores across the season plus your Hanmer round decide the season's winner. Live leaderboard, updated after every event.
